Volunteer opportunities

Join our volunteer community

EEG's non-profit businesses — restaurants, bakeries, grocery markets, handy man services and other basic services that communities need — are fully operated and managed by EEG volunteers, teachers and environmental experts that have been trained and are 100% dedicated to helping bring balance to the planet.

Seda, our community outreach representative, shows how much one person can do: she tells her neighbors and friends about our EEG projects, and her commitment and efforts have been very instrumental in funding our educational and food service projects.

Environmental Education Group (EEG) Sets Out To Harmonize The Environments On All Levels.

EEG's 50-year history and evolution is entering a new era with new understandings and new commitments. We are committed to uprooting the disharmonies and imbalances in our environments and bringing more harmony and more long term real solutions to them. Our nonprofit is dedicated to going after those things that take our planet out of balance and weaken our whole society.

Our mission

We find environmental non profits are looking for ways of healing the branches.

It is our goal to heal the roots while transplanting evolving perspectives; replacing the shallow short lived solutions and motivations; with long term strong standing complete harmonious structures; building unified wisdom & forever improving environments.

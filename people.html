Current project developers

EEG's Current Project Developers

Lielle A.

EEG President & Chairwoman of the Board

Lielle Arad is our EEG president. She infects us all with her fresh inspiration and feelings towards what she thinks environmental education should stand for. To her, environmental education is about acknowledging weaknesses in our atmospheres and finding those long-term real solutions that work to bring balance and harmony, as well as long term real security and stability to our people and to our planet. We, the members, and supporters of EEG, are proud to also be infected by her inspiration.

Lielle began to spearhead down-to-earth efforts to create tangible examples of harmonious business atmospheres to serve EEG's fundraising goals as well as to expand our community outreach. In the process, Lielle stumbled unwittingly into a web of deception and legal abuse. But Ms. Arad is not one to lay down and die. Instead, Lielle has been rising up to not only help defend EEG and herself but has become inspired to become a lawyer, and to protect our people and our planet, as well as to create access to justice through educational programs for those that may find themselves caught in these types of deceptive legal pressures and/or white-collar crimes.

As Lielle continues to work through applying her own solutions, and strengthening her own personal weaknesses, we believe that she will become an even stronger leader in helping as many people as she can rising through these painful, demoralizing, blindsiding, life-draining intimidations.

Those like Lielle, that love their humanity and planet, but lack the legal knowledge and resources to adequately defend our people and our planet and everything on it, or in it, from the many reckless layers of threats, misuse, power, greed, corruption, or chaos, will certainly understand the benefits our EEG president brings to our planet. She is our hero.

Alysa S.

Creative Education Principal · Founder of TikaKnows

Driven from a young age to better understand herself, how she fits into this world, and her environment at large, Alysa S. first became involved with EEG through her love of music to collaborate in inspiring new perspectives and raising awareness and joy through song with the musical project, b4Neptune. Her heart then began to lead her towards the world of educating, particularly little ones.

Currently under the umbrella of EEG, Alysa S. is developing a school project that we are very proud to be assisting and supporting — TikaKnows. Alysa S. is the founder and creative education principal. She teaches and writes the school's curriculum as well as writes, produces, and directs videos connected to the school's program. She is always striving to prepare more and more evolved communication techniques with the hopes of not only benefiting little ones, but people of all ages, around the planet.

The TikaKnows project is much more than a full-time commitment for her; it has become the main focus of her life. She obviously loves her work.

We are quite sure her ongoing heartfelt research and ever evolving communication and social harmonizing techniques will have a profound positive impact on our current generations and bring about major changes in our growing individuals, especially in regards to how people view themselves, their world and everything connected to it.

Seda S.

Community Outreach Representative · Secretary & Member of the Board

We would like to say a few words about our deeply dedicated wonderfully selfless, highly respected, community outreach EEG representative.

Seda is a very passionate people person. She helps everyone she can, especially the ill, and the elderly. No chore seems to be too big or small for her to do or lend a hand with.

Seda has also been helping EEG raise money for many years now. She tells her neighbors and friends about our EEG projects, and her commitment and efforts have been very instrumental in funding our educational and food service projects.

Essie Leigh

Director of Publishing

Essie has been one of EEG's crucial contributors to ‘birthing’ its more major world helping projects for over 10 years now. She is a very proud mother of 4 very bright and beautiful children, and two of her youngest children are joyful participants of TikaKnows since its inception, and her oldest, an extremely talented artist, already contributing her artistic perspectives to EEG. Although Essie's ‘formal’ education entails a bachelor's degree in Psychology from the University of Illinois at Chicago, her life experience and ‘life's work’ goes much, much deeper than that. She has been engaged in various volunteer work and CHARITY endeavors across the US and Canada ever since she was a child. Everything from homeless shelters to hospitals, not to mention groundbreaking recycling programs, and anything to help our environment and all the people in it has been her forte.

Essie has also always had a great love and talent for MUSIC, and formed B4neptune with fellow EEG member Alysa S. Together they became singer/songwriter/music producers for EEG to have a glorious and inspirational musical platform to inspire more long term real solutions through song.

Essie is EEG's Director of PUBLISHING. She was first introduced to publishing, when she was published herself in the American Journal Of Psychology in 2001, then later by the Society for American Baseball Research. She has experience in a wide range of fields, from the Modeling & Entertainment Industries, to the Corporate, Communications & Legal worlds, to the world of New Home Building & Real Estate; but it has been a great desire of hers, with blood, sweat and tears, to further support EEG's current and future undertakings every step of the way.

Essie has always done everything with sheer passion and grit and attributes her success as a mother, as a person, as a student and as a teacher, to the unwavering love and support of her fellow EEG volunteers. Rising from the ashes of great personal challenges, through her long and often winding road, debilitating physical illnesses included, it could not be more evident that she is totally inspired and completely dedicated to sharing every bit of gold ever given to her along the way, and to all those who are open to it and ready for it, in every way she possibly can.

Essie continues to inspire us all with her determined spirit and in certain ways she is like a mother to all of us.
